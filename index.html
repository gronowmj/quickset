How to play

Every clue is a straight definition. The number in brackets is the length of the answer.

Tap a square to select it, and tap it again to switch between across and down. Tap the clue bar to switch too, or use the arrows to move between clues.

Stuck? The Hint button reveals or checks letters. Your progress is saved on this device.
